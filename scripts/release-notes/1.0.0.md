First public release. Apple Silicon Macs, signed and notarized.

- Drop long videos in, click the moments you want on the filmstrip, export. Clips are trimmed, normalized and concatenated by the bundled FFmpeg in one pass.
- Live preview from background proxy encodes; drag to reorder, drag edges to trim, undo everything.
- Intro and outro title cards (30 fonts, animations), text watermark, 16:9 / 9:16 / 1:1 / 4:5 / 4:3, per-clip pan and zoom.
- Thumbnail editor: pick frames, mosaic or stripes layouts, title with scrim, PNG / JPEG / WebP / AVIF up to 4K.
- Built-in MCP server: let Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor, Codex or Gemini CLI pick the scenes and assemble the trailer live in the app. 22 tools, token-gated, localhost only.
- Everything runs on your machine. Nothing is uploaded.

**Install:** open the DMG and drag Trailers Fast to Applications. Intel Macs and Linux: build from source, see the README.
